How to play

Roll a 6 to bring a pawn out. Move clockwise around the board, then up your own coloured lane into the centre. You need an exact roll to finish. Landing on a rival pawn sends it home, unless the tile is a safe tile (start squares and stars) or the pawn has a shield. A 6, a capture or reaching home gives a bonus roll. Three 6s in a row forfeits the turn.

Orbs spawn on the track. Land exactly on one to collect a power card (you can hold three). Cards are played before you roll.
